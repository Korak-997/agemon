# Tarn & Ember design

Warm, unhurried, tactile. Paper-and-roast tones, never stark white.

- Display: Fraunces. Body: Source Sans 3. Tasting notes set in italics.
- Colour: roast brown ink on oat paper, one ember-orange accent for actions only.
- Shape: soft 10px corners, no drop shadows, thin 1px rules to separate content.
- Voice: plain and specific. Say "washed Ethiopian, blueberry and tea", not "premium coffee".
